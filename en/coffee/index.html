About

It started with one machine in a kitchen

In 2017 we roasted beans at a summer house and sold them to friends by subscription. A year later we opened a 24-seat room, put the roaster in the corner and stopped hiding the process: you can watch the roast from your table.

We don't chase speed. The barista will ask which flavour you prefer and won't mind remaking your drink.
