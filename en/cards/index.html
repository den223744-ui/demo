One important note about Amazon

Amazon does not allow text, badges or logos on the main image (position 1): it must be the product alone on a pure white background. Slides like these belong in positions 2–7 and in A+ content. I prepare a clean main image for Amazon separately, at no extra cost. On Etsy, eBay and Shopify text is allowed on every image, including the first one.
